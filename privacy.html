Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

In short: NITRO RIVALS does not require an account, email address or phone number. Your career progress, garage and settings are stored on your device. If you use online features (friends and online races), a small amount of game data is stored on our game server. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob.

This policy explains what data NITRO RIVALS (“the game”), published by DigiNova Labs (“we”), uses on iPhone, iPad and Android devices.

1. Data stored on your device

Your player name, level, credits, tokens, unlocked cars, upgrades, career stars, best times and settings are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data.

2. Online features (friends and online races)

When the game connects to our server it creates a random, anonymous player identifier on your device. To make friends and online races work, our server stores:

  • the anonymous player identifier and a random key that proves it belongs to your device,
  • your friend code, the player name you chose, your level and selected car,
  • your friend list and pending friend requests,
  • your online status (online, in a lobby, racing) and when you were last online.

During an online race, your car’s position and speed are relayed in real time to the other players in the same room. Race positions are not stored after the race. Online data is used only to provide these features. It is not sold and not used for advertising.

Deleting online data: in the game open Settings › “Delete online data”. This immediately and permanently deletes your friend code, player name, friend list and online account from our server. Your progress on the device is kept.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads in menus and full-screen ads when a race starts or ends. Ads are never shown during a race. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads.

  • On iPhone and iPad, personalized ads use the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google’s consent form before personalized ads are shown.
  • Opt-in rewarded videos (for example “double rewards”) are shown only when you choose to watch them.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. In-app purchases and the “Remove Ads” subscription

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store), Huawei (AppGallery) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. The game stores which items you own on your device so they can be restored. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your store account settings.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad requests are configured to exclude sensitive and mature ad categories.

6. Data retention and security

Online account data is kept while you use online features and is deleted when you use “Delete online data”. Inactive online accounts may be deleted after 24 months. Connections to our server are encrypted (TLS).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Most of this you can do directly in the game; for anything else, contact us below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
