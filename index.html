FAQ

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings or the Shop in the game and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same store account you bought with.

How do I cancel “Remove Ads”?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › NITRO RIVALS. You keep ad-free play until the end of the paid period.

How do I delete my online data?

In the game open Settings › “Delete online data”. This permanently removes your friend code, player name and friend list from our server.

Online races don’t connect.

Check your internet connection and try again. Online racing needs a connection; career and quick races work offline.
